# DPDP Act 2023 & DPDP Rules 2025 – Master Overview & Router

Official Primary Sources:
- Statute: Act No. 22 of 2023, Assent 11 August 2023, Gazette of India Extraordinary, Part II Sec. 1.
- Subordinate Rules: Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 (Notified 13 November 2025; Corrigendum 16 December 2025).
- Commencement Notification: G.S.R. 843(E) dated 13 November 2025.
- Board Establishment: G.S.R. 844(E) dated 13 November 2025.

## 1. Statutory Response Structure for Legal Questions

When responding to legal queries on India's data protection regime, AI agents must structure answers using this consistent framework:
1. **Legal Provision**: Exact Section of the Act and corresponding Rule of the DPDP Rules, 2025.
2. **Plain-Language Interpretation**: Plain English breakdown of what the text requires.
3. **Applicable DPDP Rule**: Procedural standards, forms, language requirements, or thresholds.
4. **Commencement Status**: State clearly whether the provision is In Force (Phase 1), Commencing Soon (Phase 2), or Future Commencement (Phase 3).
5. **Compliance Implications**: Technical, operational, and organizational actions required.
6. **Exceptions & Exemptions**: Applicable carve-outs under Section 17 or specific provisos.
7. **Official Source References**: Citations to Gazette notifications and statutory paragraphs.
8. **Legal Uncertainty & Interpretive Limitations**: Explicitly flag points subject to executive notification or judicial interpretation.

## 2. Phased Enforcement Timeline (Current as of 5 November 2026)

Under Notification G.S.R. 843(E), the Act is enforced in three distinct phases:

| Enforcement Phase | Effective Date | Status (as of 5 Nov 2026) | Provisions Brought into Force |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Phase 1** | 13 November 2025 | **IN FORCE** | Section 1 (Short title), Section 2 (All definitions), Sections 18–26 (Board establishment, structure, qualifications), Section 30 (Consolidated Fund), Section 34 (MeitY info powers), Sections 40–41 (Rule-making), Section 43 (Removal of difficulties), and Rules 1, 2, 17–21. |
| **Phase 2** | 13 November 2026 | **COMMENCING IN 8 DAYS** | Section 6(9) (Consent Manager registration & obligations), Section 27(1)(d) (Board inquiry into Consent Managers), and Rule 4 (Consent Manager technical & operational standards). |
| **Phase 3** | 13 May 2027 | **FUTURE COMMENCEMENT** | Sections 3–17 (Fiduciary duties, notice, consent, legitimate uses, child data, SDF, Principal rights, cross-border, exemptions), Sections 27(1)(a)-(c), 28–29, 31–33, 35–39, 42, 44, the Schedule (Penalties), and Rules 3, 5–16, 22–23. |

> **Critical Agent Instruction**: Never present Phase 3 obligations (such as Section 8 security safeguards or Section 11 access rights) as currently enforceable statutory liabilities today. Advise clients that Phase 3 establishes a preparation runway culminating on 13 May 2027.

## 4. Scope and Applicability (Section 3)

### Applies To:
1. **Digital Personal Data Processing Within India**:
   - Personal data collected in digital form.
   - Personal data collected offline in non-digital form and subsequently digitized.
2. **Extraterritorial Processing (Outside India)**:
   - Processing carried out outside India if such processing is in connection with any activity related to offering goods or services to Data Principals within the territory of India (Section 3(b)).

### Does Not Apply To (Statutory Carve-outs):
1. **Personal or Domestic Purposes**: Personal data processed by an individual for personal or domestic purposes (Section 3(c)(i)).
2. **Publicly Available Personal Data**: Personal data that is made or caused to be made publicly available by:
   - The Data Principal to whom such personal data relates (e.g. self-published blog, public social profile).
   - Any other person who is under an obligation under any law for the time being in force in India to make such personal data publicly available (e.g., corporate filings on MCA portal, electoral rolls).

## 5. Key Definitions (Section 2) – In Force Since 13 Nov 2025

- **Data Fiduciary (s.2(i))**: Any person who alone or in conjunction with other persons determines the purpose and means of processing personal data.
- **Data Principal (s.2(j))**: The individual to whom the personal data relates. Where the individual is a child (under 18), includes parents or lawful guardian; where a person with disability, includes lawful guardian.
- **Data Processor (s.2(k))**: Any person who processes personal data on behalf of a Data Fiduciary.
- **Digital Personal Data (s.2(n))**: Personal data in digital form.
- **Personal Data (s.2(t))**: Any data about an individual who is identifiable by or in relation to such data.
- **Processing (s.2(x))**: Wholly or partly automated operation or set of operations performed on digital personal data (collection, recording, organization, structuring, storage, adaptation, retrieval, use, alignment, sharing, erasure, destruction).
- **Consent Manager (s.2(g))**: A person registered with the Board that acts as a single point of contact to enable a Data Principal to give, manage, review, and withdraw her consent through an accessible, transparent, and interoperable platform.
- **Significant Data Fiduciary (s.2(z))**: Any Data Fiduciary or class of Data Fiduciaries as may be notified by the Central Government under Section 10 based on statutory risk assessment criteria.
- **Personal Data Breach (s.2(u))**: Any unauthorized processing of personal data or accidental disclosure, acquisition, sharing, use, alteration, destruction, or loss of access to personal data, that compromises the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of personal data.
- **Board (s.2(c))**: The Data Protection Board of India established under Section 18.
- **Appellate Tribunal (s.2(b))**: The Telecom Disputes Settlement and Appellate Tribunal (TDSAT) established under Section 14 of the TRAI Act, 1997.
- **She / Her (s.2(2))**: The Act uses the feminine pronoun ("she", "her") to refer to an individual of any gender.

## 6. General Answering Workflow for Agents

1. **Classify Role**: Determine whether the entity is a Data Fiduciary, Data Processor, Significant Data Fiduciary, Consent Manager, or Data Principal.
2. **Evaluate Scope (s.3)**: Confirm whether data is digital personal data and processed in India or connected to offering goods/services to individuals in India.
3. **Verify Exemptions (s.17)**: Check if judicial, law enforcement, research, or startup exemptions apply.
4. **Check Enforcement Phase**: Confirm whether the applicable provision is in Phase 1, Phase 2, or Phase 3.
5. **Route to Target Skill**: Delegate detailed analysis to `dpdp-consent-and-notice`, `dpdp-fiduciary-obligations`, `dpdp-data-principal-rights`, or `dpdp-board-enforcement`.
6. **Cite Exact Authority**: Ground answers in specific sections of the Act, clauses of the DPDP Rules 2025, and relevant Gazette notifications.
