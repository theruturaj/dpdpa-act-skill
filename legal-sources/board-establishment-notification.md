# Legal Source: Data Protection Board of India Establishment

## Notification Metadata
- **Notification Number**: G.S.R. 844(E)
- **Authority**: Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY), Government of India
- **Statutory Power**: Section 18(1) of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023
- **Date of Notification**: 13 November 2025
- **Gazette Reference**: Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (i)
- **Verification Date**: 2026-11-05

## Summary of Provisions
1. **Establishment as Body Corporate**:
   - Establishes the **Data Protection Board of India** with effect from 13 November 2025 as a body corporate having perpetual succession and a common seal, with power to acquire, hold, and dispose of property, and to sue or be sued.
2. **Headquarters & Regional Benches**:
   - Designates the principal seat/headquarters of the Board at the National Capital Region (NCR), New Delhi.
   - Authorizes digital hearings, telepresence, and electronic proceedings across India.
3. **Institutional Character**:
   - Digital-by-design institution: electronic filing of complaints, digital summons, secure online exchange of evidence, and publication of speaking orders through secure digital portals.
